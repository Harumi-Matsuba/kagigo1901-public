# The 1901 numeric kana cipher: table and telegram-by-telegram correspondence

日本語: [READING1901.md](READING1901.md)

## System
- Inside a telegram written in romanised Japanese, only the sensitive words (names of persons and places, titles) are put into digits. Two digits stand for one kana. For transmission the digits are cut into groups of five, so the last group of a run is shorter (`00161 21108` = 5 pairs, `45671 867` = 4 pairs).
- The table is the kana syllabary taken vowel row by vowel row (a-row, i-row, u-row, e-row, o-row), with each voiced and half-voiced kana placed directly after its plain kana, numbered from 00.

| Numbers | Kana |
|---|---|
| 00-14 | ア カ ガ サ ザ タ ダ ナ ハ バ パ マ ヤ ラ ワ |
| 15-26 | イ キ ギ シ ジ チ ニ ヒ ビ ピ ミ リ |
| 27-39 | ウ ク グ ス ズ ツ ヌ フ ブ プ ム ユ ル |
| 40-52 | エ ケ ゲ セ ゼ テ デ ネ ヘ ベ ペ メ レ |
| 53-67 | オ コ ゴ ソ ゾ ト ド ノ ホ ボ ポ モ ヨ ロ ン |

- 38 values are attested in the telegrams below. The other 30 are filled in from the rule of the arrangement and are not attested. The use of 68-99 is not known ([OPEN.en.md](OPEN.en.md)).
- The particle を is written オ (53); the particle は is written ハ (08) or ワ (14).

### Two telegrams the table was applied to after it was fixed
| Reference, frame | Date, sender | Digits | Reading with the table | Contemporary translation |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| C09122796200 fr. 0071 | 8 Mar 1901, Chungking, Capt. Idogawa to Fukushima | Sisensootok kwaiken `53645 83718 16530 05329` | オモトムシキオアオグ | 四川総督会見ヲ求ム指揮ヲ仰グ (the Viceroy of Szechwan asks for an interview; request instructions) |
| C09122806100 fr. 0245-0246 | 18 Feb 1901, Hiroshima, Maj.-Gen. Manabe to Tamura (kana telegram) | ジウブンナル `三四二八三七` ニワ | フクム | 充分ナル服務ニハ (full duty) |
| 〃 | 〃 | ワ `六九六七二〇一四` テキトウ | [69]ンチワ | 任地ハ適当ト (the post is suitable) |
| 〃 | 〃 | コタヘ〇 `四五六七一八六五二八九一` 一 イケンヲ | テンシヨク[91] | 石橋転任ノ事ニ就キ意見 (opinion on Ishibashi's transfer) |
| 〃 | 〃 | ビヨウジヨウ `九一` ワ | [91] | 病状ニ就テハ (as to his illness) |

- モ (64), ト (58) and グ (29) in C09122796200 occur in no other telegram; they were fixed beforehand by the rule of the arrangement.
- C09122806100 is the telegram given as "not read" in the October 2026 version. ン チ ワ フ ク ム テ シ ヨ follow the table. 69 and 91 are outside the table. 91 stands both times where the translation has ニ就キ / ニ就テ ("concerning": 転職[91] = 転任ノ事ニ就キ, 病状[91]ワ = 病状ニ就テハ) and looks like a code for that word (conjecture; Satoshi Tomokiyo gives this alignment at the group level in meijiarmy2.htm). 69 stands where 任地 has ニ (the table's ニ is 21). The "〇" at the head of the first run and the "一" at its end are left over when the run is cut into pairs ([OPEN.en.md](OPEN.en.md)). The translation says 転任 (transfer), the digits read テンシヨク (転職).

## The five drafts sent out by the General Staff, same layout
Five drafts from the General Staff (Maj.-Gen. Tamura) to Maj.-Gen. Fukushima (C09122808300, C09122808400, C09122809400, C09122810100, C09122812300) have the same layout, but this table does not read them. On 2026-11-10 they were found to be read by a different table (a grid: tens digit = vowel row, units digit = consonant column). Table and reading: [READING1901B.en.md](READING1901B.en.md).
