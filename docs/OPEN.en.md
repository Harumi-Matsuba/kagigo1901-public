# Open points

## 1. C09122806100 (1901-02-18, Hiroshima) cannot be read
- Four groups of digits (S1 14 digits, S2 2 digits, S3 8 digits, S4 6 digits). From the decoded text and the surrounding plain text, the portion each covers is determined: S2 = ニ, S3 = ニンチワ, S4 = フクム. Taking 2 digits as 1 kana, the lengths fit.
- Tried and not fitting: the 1902 table; the 1902 table with a periodic digit (period 1–7) added (including the case where the key is restarted for each group); a rule in which one of the two digits represents the vowel; shifts of the numbering in iroha order or gojūon order.
- The same ニ appears as 91 and as 69. Because the values of all 15 pairs differ, this does not contradict a table that assigns several values to one character (homophones), but there is also no power to test that.
- A second telegram using the same method was not found even after viewing every image of the Meiji 34 telegram file.
- Beside the digits there are faint marks in a different hand. Whether they are dakuten or collation marks by the receiving side cannot be decided from the binarized image.

## 2. C09122909900 does not reach a full reading
- The lowest value in the independent second transcription is 84.6% (Claude Sonnet, one run on the enlarged image). In that run, a small エ-shaped marking at the lower right of 「三」 in column 1 was entered as one character of the text, and the head of column 2 and the 八 in column 4 were also read as different characters. The other runs are 88.5–100%.
- The horizontal stroke at the head of column 2 is hidden where its right end enters a thick vertical band of ink. Whether it is 一 (the head of ン = 10), or フ or 七, cannot be decided from the image.
- イ is written as 17 (19 in the table). The image also shows 七. Taken to be an error by the writer or in transmission (conjecture).

## 3. C03020059400 does not reach a full reading
- Two pairs do not agree with the decoded text: 09 (04 if the plain text is ハ) and 88 (68 if the plain text is ジ). The image shows 〇九 and 八八.
- The last character of column 1, compared with the 九 of the same writer, has an upright vertical stroke and a rounded right stroke. It was suspected to be 四, and Codex (twice) and Sonnet (twice) were made to judge it beside samples, but in all four runs the first candidate was 九. It is kept as 九, and the disagreement with the decoded text is taken to be an error by the writer or in transmission (conjecture).
- If it were decided to be 四, the agreement with the decoded text would be 11/12. This cannot be decided without the original.

## 4. The name
- It has not been decided which of Yakugō (鑰号), Kongō (坤号) and the 「数字ノ分」 (numeral part) of Tokugō (特号) this table is. No text on their use was found in JACAR; the physical items held by the National Institute for Defense Studies would have to be seen.

## 5. Scope
- Items in the 1904 files of the Army Ministry Daijiki that did not match the JACAR search for 「訳文」 or 「電報訳」 have not been viewed. No numeric telegram of 1903 was found in the Meiji 36 telegram file (catalogue).
