# 1901, five drafts from the General Staff to Maj.-Gen. Fukushima: table and reading

日本語: [READING1901B.md](READING1901B.md)

## System
- Same layout as the incoming telegrams ([READING1901.en.md](READING1901.en.md)): inside a telegram in romanised Japanese only the sensitive words are turned into digits, two digits per kana, sent in groups of five. All 23 digit runs have even length.
- The table is not the one of the incoming telegrams. It is **a grid: tens digit = vowel row (dan), units digit = consonant column (gyō)**.

| | tens digit | units digit |
|---|---|---|
| lower half 01–50 | 0 a, 1 i, 2 u, 3 e, 4 o | 1 ア (vowel), 2 k, 3 g, 4 s, 5 z, 6 t, 7 d, 8 n, 9 h, 0 b (the b column is 10, 20, 30, 40, 50) |
| upper half 51–95 | 5 a, 6 i, 7 u, 8 e, 9 o | 1 p, 2 m, 3 y, 4 r. 55 = ワ (wa), 65 = ン (n) |

- Examples: ア01 カ02 バ10 シ14 ウ21 オ41 ノ48 ボ50 パ51 マ52 ワ55 ミ62 リ64 ン65 ヨ93.
- The particle ヲ is sent as オ (41).
- Sino-Japanese words are spelled in the historical kana of the time: 条約 = ゼウヤク, 張 = テウ, 帰朝 = キテウ, 忠告 = チウコク, 膠州 = コウシウ.
- 53 different values occur. 48 of them are in the table above; **five values (59, 60, 80, 89, 99; ten occurrences) are not**. All five have units digit 9 or 0 in the upper half and are codes for words (see "Word codes" below).
- The 22 table values that do not occur in the telegrams are filled in from the grid rule and are not confirmed by any telegram. The use of units digits 6–8 in the upper half and of 75, 85, 95 is unknown.

## Word codes
| value | word | where | basis |
|---|---|---|---|
| 99 | 露 (Russia) | `[99][50]ミツ…ノ furiekinaru`, `[99][60]ゼウハ` | context (Russo-Chinese secret agreement / articles) |
| 60 | 清 (China) | same, and `リコウシヨウハ[60][80] osite` | context and the record below |
| 80 | 公使 (minister, i.e. envoy) | `[60][80] osite`, `[89][80]ノ`, `[80]ガ` | the record below |
| 89 | 独 (Germany) — conjecture | `[89][80]ノ … how to receive him` | one occurrence; taken as the minister of the Field Marshal's (Waldersee's) country |
| 59 | 政府 (government) — conjecture | `[60][80] osite [59]ニ tugesasuru`, `waga [59]ニ テウシドウ yori` | context; 外務省 (Foreign Ministry) or 外務大臣 (Foreign Minister) would also fit |

- **Record**: the Foreign Ministry file 外務大臣ノ会談要領雑件 第二巻 contains the minutes of the Chinese Minister's calls of 1 and 4 March 1901 (JACAR B03030000800, frame 0061): "1 March. The Chinese Minister in Japan came to the Ministry and showed a copy of a telegram he had received from Prince Qing and Li Hongzhang". The telegram C09122809400 of 2 March, "Li Hongzhang had [60][80] tell [59] that …", reports this on the following day. The table of contents of the same volume (B03030000700, frames 0053–0054) lists a telegram from Liu [Kunyi] and Zhang [Zhidong] asking the Empire to warn Russia, which matches "Zhang Zhidong has asked … that this treaty should not come into being". The table of contents abbreviates the envoys as 清公使, 独公使.
- For `[80]ガ` in the telegram of 1 May: a draft to Lt.-Gen. Yamaguchi of the same day (C09122812200, catalogue transcription) says "the Government has telegraphed its opinion on the reduction of troops to Minister Komura".
- The first kana of the five words (ロ, ド, コ, シ, セ) are in iroha order while the values fall: 99, 89, 80, 60, 59. This fits a word list in iroha order numbered from the top down (noticed after the words were assigned; it breaks if 59 is 外務省).
- The catalogue entries of the same binder were fetched for C09122807700–C09122816000 (84 items). The only "cipher telegrams to Maj.-Gen. Fukushima" are these five.

## Reading
[ ] = value not in the table (word code). 〔 〕 = value in the table, but the kana is doubtful as part of a word. The romaji includes spellings re-read after the digits were read (the first transcription is in [data/outgoing1901.txt](../data/outgoing1901.txt)). Source for all five: JACAR, National Institute for Defense Studies, 参謀本部 雑, 『自明治３４年１月５日至同年６月２９日 通報綴』. No contemporary plaintext of these drafts has been found.

### C09122808300 (February 1901, undated; frames 0306–0307)
| digits | reading with the table |
|---|---|
| `02624 14832 4221` yurusazu | カミオノ〔ケ〕コウ (… 許サズ) |
| `1982 1518` yarutumori | ヒメジニ (遣ル積リ) |

"Kamio's 〔kekō〕 is not permitted; we intend to send him to Himeji." 32 (ケ) is clearly 32 in the image. ケコウ is taken as ケンコウ (健康, health) with ン (65) dropped (conjecture): "Kamio's health does not permit; we intend to send him to Himeji." A draft of 20 February in the same binder (C09122808700, catalogue transcription) uses the same turn of phrase about another officer's health. Kamio Mitsuomi became chief of staff of the 10th Division (headquarters at Himeji) in February 1901 (Japanese Wikipedia, 神尾光臣).

### C09122808400 (14 February 1901, No. 21; frames 0308–0309)
| digits | reading with the table |
|---|---|
| `41411 45248` kotowaima soodantiu | オオシマノ (事ハ今相談中) |
| mosiikeneba `22244 83409` ikaga | クスノセハ (如何) |
| `14100 94618` kaku kaesu Tamura | シバハトニ (角返ス) |

"The matter of Ōshima is under discussion now. If he cannot go, how about Kusunose? Shiba will be sent back in any case. Tamura."

### C09122809400 (2 March 1901; frames 0331–0332)
| digits | reading with the table |
|---|---|
| `99506 22652 2248` furiekinaru | [99]〔ボ〕ミツ〔マ〕クノ (不利益ナル) |
| `5503` tiukokunitaisi | ワガ (忠告ニ対シ) |
| `64422 11493 2109 6080` osite | リコウシヨウハ[60][80] (ヲシテ) |
| `5918` tugesasuruni | [59]ニ (告ゲサスルニ) |
| `99603 52109` motukano… riekito kangoureba | [99][60]ゼウハ (目下ノ … 利益ト考フレバ) |
| `43962 14222 09118 40306` kii omotte… | ゴ〔チ〕ウコクハイレガタ (キ意ヲ以テ …) |
| mata waga `5918 36211 44721` yorikono | [59]ニテウシドウ (ヨリ此ノ) |
| `35215 32248` naritatanuyoo nito tanomi kitareri | ゼウヤクノ (成リ立タヌ様ニト頼ミ来レリ) |
| mata `34110 16598 6492` onadi imino | セイアン〔ヨ〕リモ (同ジ意味ノ) |
| `37659 12112` tari Tamura | デンポウキ (タリ) |

"To our advice that the Russo-〔Chinese〕 secret agreement is disadvantageous, Li Hongzhang had the Chinese Minister tell [the Government?] that, considering the Russo-Chinese articles advantageous in the present …, he is of a mind that the advice is hard to accept …. Also, Zhang Zhidong has asked our [Government?] that this treaty should not come into being. Also a telegram of the same meaning has come from Xi'an. Tamura." (In the margin: "Send the full text of this telegram to Divisional Commander Yamaguchi as well.")

- The first group is taken as 99506: first transcription 99506; the second reader (Codex) read 77306 from the whole message field and 99506 from a strip of this line alone; a third reader (Claude Sonnet) read 99506. The JACAR catalogue transcription has 99606. 50 gives ボ, which makes no word, and `[99][60]ゼウ` follows later, so 50 is taken as a slip for 60 (清), giving 露清密約, the Russo-Chinese secret agreement (conjecture; not counted as read).
- In 22652 the pair 52 gives マ. With ヤ (53) the word would be ミツヤク (密約, secret agreement). The image looks like 2. Taken as a slip of one digit (conjecture).
- 〔チ〕 and 〔ヨ〕 come from digits re-read after the table was known (43962→43162, 16598→16593). The second reader, who did not know the table, read 43962 and 16598, so these two are treated as conjecture and not counted as read. Re-reading from strips did not support them either: Codex 4396{2/4} and 96598, Sonnet 43{6/5}… and 16598.

### C09122810100 (8 March 1901; frames 0348–0350)
| digits | reading with the table |
|---|---|
| `33652 41109 42211 42155 65936 41849 6518` kitaru nozomi{o/a} | ゲンスイハコウシウワンヨリニホンニ (来タル望ミ…) |
| `92168 98048` te?oriikani | 〔モチ〕[89][80]ノ (… 如何ニ) |
| `02844 11912` ukurukao saguritiunari | カレオヒキ (受クルカヲ探リ中ナリ) |
| `55840 94421` tooni hikiukuru | ワレハソウ (当ニ引キ受クル) |
| `93212 40884 09626 57321` wo susumerareyo Tamura | ヨウスナレハ〔ミ〕ンユウ (ヲ勧メラレヨ) |

"The Field Marshal 〔has〕 the wish to come to Japan from Kiaochow Bay and is sounding out, 〔through〕 the [German?] Minister, how he would be received. As it looks as though we shall receive him suitably, recommend the 〔pleasure〕 trip to him. Tamura."

- The third group of the first run and the second group of the fourth run were 42291 and 94427 in the first transcription, and were re-read as 42211 and 94421 after the table was known. The second reader (Codex), not knowing the table, read 42211 and 94421.
- The romaji next to the digits is read differently by different readers. After the first run: nozomio (Claude's re-reading, Codex from a strip, the JACAR catalogue transcription) or nozomia (Sonnet). After the second run: teyoriikani (Claude's re-reading, Codex from a strip), tezoriikani (Sonnet), tugoiikani (Codex from the whole field). With nozomio and teyori the text is 望ミヲ持チ ("has the wish") and 手ヨリ ("through the hand of"), and モチ becomes a word; as the readers disagree, モチ is not counted as read.
- In 09626 the pair 62 gives ミ. With マ (52) the word would be マンユウ (漫遊, pleasure trip). The image looks like 62. Taken as a slip of one digit (conjecture); a draft in the same binder (C09122808100) speaks of his 来遊 (visit) to Japan on the way home.
- "The Field Marshal" is taken to be Count Waldersee. Fukushima's telegram of 22 April (C09122801000) has "the Field Marshal has received the Emperor's permission to go to Japan".

### C09122812300 (1 May 1901; frames 0412–0414)
| digits | reading with the table |
|---|---|
| `12362 12474` yooni … nareri | キテウスル (様ニ … ナレリ) |
| mata `42210 61139 11482 42109 8003` Ki{b/k}arazareba | コウタイヘイノスウハ[80]ガ (… ザレバ) |
| `48456 241` mitasigatasi Tamura | ノゾミオ (充タシ難シ) |

"It has come about that … returns home. Also, as to the number of relief troops, unless the Minister …, the wish is hard to meet. Tamura."

- The romaji after `[80]ガ` is Kibarazareba (Claude's re-reading, Sonnet) or Kikarazareba (Codex); 気張ラザレバ ("unless he presses hard") and 決マラザレバ ("unless it is settled") have been considered, and the word is left open. The word after yooni is kobo (Codex, Sonnet); what it stands for is not known.
