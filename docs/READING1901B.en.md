# 1901, five drafts from the General Staff to Maj.-Gen. Fukushima: table and reading

日本語: [READING1901B.md](READING1901B.md)

## System
- Same layout as the incoming telegrams ([READING1901.en.md](READING1901.en.md)): inside a telegram in romanised Japanese only the sensitive words are turned into digits, two digits per kana, sent in groups of five. All 23 digit runs have even length.
- The table is not the one of the incoming telegrams. It is **a grid: tens digit = vowel row (dan), units digit = consonant column (gyō)**.

| | tens digit | units digit |
|---|---|---|
| lower half 01–50 | 0 a, 1 i, 2 u, 3 e, 4 o | 1 ア (vowel), 2 k, 3 g, 4 s, 5 z, 6 t, 7 d, 8 n, 9 h, 0 b (the b column is 10, 20, 30, 40, 50) |
| upper half 51–95 | 5 a, 6 i, 7 u, 8 e, 9 o | 1 p, 2 m, 3 y, 4 r. 55 = ワ (wa), 65 = ン (n) |

- Examples: ア01 カ02 バ10 シ14 ウ21 オ41 ノ48 ボ50 パ51 マ52 ワ55 ミ62 リ64 ン65 ヨ93.
- The particle ヲ is sent as オ (41).
- Sino-Japanese words are spelled in the historical kana of the time: 条約 = ゼウヤク, 張 = テウ, 帰朝 = キテウ, 忠告 = チウコク, 膠州 = コウシウ.
- 53 different values occur. 48 of them are in the table above; **five values (59, 60, 80, 89, 99; ten occurrences) are not**. All five have units digit 9 or 0 in the upper half and look like codes for words such as countries or offices (conjecture; [OPEN.en.md](OPEN.en.md)).
- The 22 table values that do not occur in the telegrams are filled in from the grid rule and are not confirmed by any telegram. The use of units digits 6–8 in the upper half and of 75, 85, 95 is unknown.

## Reading
[ ] = value not in the table. 〔 〕 = value in the table, but the kana is doubtful as part of a word. The romaji includes spellings re-read after the digits were read (the first transcription is in [data/outgoing1901.txt](../data/outgoing1901.txt)). Source for all five: JACAR, National Institute for Defense Studies, 参謀本部 雑, 『自明治３４年１月５日至同年６月２９日 通報綴』. No contemporary plaintext of these drafts has been found.

### C09122808300 (February 1901, undated; frames 0306–0307)
| digits | reading with the table |
|---|---|
| `02624 14832 4221` yurusazu | カミオノ〔ケ〕コウ (… 許サズ) |
| `1982 1518` yarutumori | ヒメジニ (遣ル積リ) |

"Kamio's 〔kekō〕 is not permitted; we intend to send him to Himeji." 32 (ケ) is clearly 32 in the image; what word ケコウ stands for is not known.

### C09122809400 (2 March 1901; frames 0331–0332)
| digits | reading with the table |
|---|---|
| `99506 22652 2248` furiekinaru | [99]〔ボ〕ミツ〔マ〕クノ (不利益ナル) |
| `5503` tiukokunitaisi | ワガ (忠告ニ対シ) |
| `64422 11493 2109 6080` osite | リコウシヨウハ[60][80] (ヲシテ) |
| `5918` tugesasuruni | [59]ニ (告ゲサスルニ) |
| `99603 52109` motukano… riekito kangoureba | [99][60]ゼウハ (目下ノ … 利益ト考フレバ) |
| `43962 14222 09118 40306` kii omotte… | ゴ〔チ〕ウコクハイレガタ (キ意ヲ以テ …) |
| mata waga `5918 36211 44721` yorikono | [59]ニテウシドウ (ヨリ此ノ) |
| `35215 32248` naritatanuyoo nito tanomi kitareri | ゼウヤクノ (成リ立タヌ様ニト頼ミ来レリ) |
| mata `34110 16598 6492` onadi imino | セイアン〔ヨ〕リモ (同ジ意味ノ) |
| `37659 12112` tari Tamura | デンポウキ (タリ) |

"… to our advice that the … secret agreement is disadvantageous, Li Hongzhang had [60][80] tell [59] that, [99][60] … considering it advantageous in the present …, he is of a mind that the advice is hard to accept …. Also, Zhang Zhidong has asked our [59] that this treaty should not come into being. Also a telegram of the same meaning has come from Xi'an. Tamura." (In the margin: "Send the full text of this telegram to Divisional Commander Yamaguchi as well.")

- The digits of the first group are not settled: first transcription 99506, second reader (Codex) 77306; under magnification the third digit is 3 or 5.
- In 22652 the pair 52 gives マ. With ヤ (53) the word would be ミツヤク (密約, secret agreement). The image looks like 2.
- 〔チ〕 and 〔ヨ〕 come from digits re-read after the table was known (43962→43162, 16598→16593). The second reader, who did not know the table, read 43962 and 16598, so these two are treated as conjecture and not counted as read.

### C09122810100 (8 March 1901; frames 0348–0350)
| digits | reading with the table |
|---|---|
| `33652 41109 42211 42155 65936 41849 6518` kitaru nozomi a… | ゲンスイハコウシウワンヨリニホンニ (来タル望ミア…) |
| `92168 98048` tugoo ikani | 〔モチ〕[89][80]ノ (都合如何ニ) |
| `02844 11912` ukurukao saguritiunari | カレオヒキ (受クルカヲ探リ中ナリ) |
| `55840 94421` tooni hikiukuru | ワレハソウ (当ニ引キ受クル) |
| `93212 40884 09626 57321` wo susumerareyo Tamura | ヨウスナレハ〔ミ〕ンユウ (ヲ勧メラレヨ) |

"The Field Marshal wishes to come to Japan from Kiaochow Bay …. We are sounding out how 〔…〕[89][80] would be placed to receive him. If it looks as though we shall receive him suitably, recommend the 〔pleasure〕 trip to him. Tamura."

- The third group of the first run and the second group of the fourth run were 42291 and 94427 in the first transcription, and were re-read as 42211 and 94421 after the table was known. The second reader (Codex), not knowing the table, read 42211 and 94421.
- In 09626 the pair 62 gives ミ. With マ (52) the word would be マンユウ (漫遊, pleasure trip). The image looks like 62.
- "The Field Marshal" is taken to be Count Waldersee. Fukushima's telegram of 22 April (C09122801000) has "the Field Marshal has received the Emperor's permission to go to Japan".
