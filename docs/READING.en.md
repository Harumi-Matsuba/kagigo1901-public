# Readings (telegram by telegram)

All sources are from JACAR (Japan Center for Asian Historical Records; held by the National Institute for Defense Studies, Ministry of Defense). Frame numbers (コマ番号) are the serial numbers at the lower right of the image. The transcription of the digits is in `data/transcription.txt`.

## 1. C09122911500 — 1902-03-27, Osaka, Chief of the General Staff Ōyama → Lieutenant General Terauchi (full reading)
- File: General Staff Office, Miscellaneous (Confidential), 『明治３５年 電報綴 北清事件』 (Telegram file, Meiji 35, North China Incident). Title: 「（大坂大山総長より）本職帰京迄本部の事務は貴官宜敷取計はれ度件」 (From Chief Ōyama at Osaka: request that you handle the business of the headquarters until my return to Tokyo). 5 frames (0108–0112).
- Telegram form (frame 0110): sending office ヲウサカ (Osaka), No. 987, 27 March, received 6:25 p.m., 72 characters. The text consists only of kanji numerals, 10 characters × 7 columns plus 「八〇」. At the lower right of the digits, a black fill and a small dot alternate every 2 characters (taken to be marks separating the 2-digit groups; conjecture).
- Digits (by 2 digits): 44 10 17 42 28 18 18 42 29 03 87 44 10 76 45 68 23 04 18 28 00 10 42 41 17 28 46 11 04 08 01 04 31 06 17 ｜ 80
- Reading: ホ ン シ ヨ ク キ キ ヨ ウ マ デ ホ ン ブ ノ ジ ム ハ キ ク ワ ン ヨ ロ シ ク ト リ ハ カ ラ ハ レ タ シ
- Contemporary decoded text (frame 0108): 「本職帰京迄本部ノ事務ハ貴官宜シク取計ハヲレタシ」 (Until I return to Tokyo, please handle the business of the headquarters as you see fit). All 35 pairs agree.
- Note: the transcription in the content field of the JACAR catalogue drops one 18 (in the image, the head of the second column is 一八一八).

## 2. C09123116600 — 1904-01-25, Kokura → General Staff Office, Major General Iguchi (full reading, no decoded text)
- File: General Staff Office, Miscellaneous (Confidential), 『明治３７年１月２７日より明治３７年３月１日まで 臨号書類綴 自臨第２５１号至第４８１号 ２冊の内２ 台戦第３号』. Title: 「韓国５万分１地図５０部韓国満洲１００万分１地図２０部送付」 (Sending 50 copies of the 1:50,000 map of Korea and 20 copies of the 1:1,000,000 map of Korea and Manchuria). 2 frames (0025: draft of the reply; 0026: telegram form).
- Telegram form (frame 0026): sending office コクラ (Kokura), No. 62, 25 January, character-count field 四十 (40). Designation field 「ラカ」.
- Text: コロクニシ ｜ 42 42 29 45 08 10 48 28 16 78 57 19 42 ｜ サレタシ ヲハラ
- Reading: ヨ ヨ ウ ノ カ ン コ ク チ ズ ダ イ ヨ → 「（要用ノ）韓国地図貸与サレタシ」 (Please lend [the necessary] maps of Korea). 「ヨヨウ」 (for 要用, which would be ヨウヨウ) and 「ダイヨ」 (for 貸与, which would be タイヨ) are taken to be the sender's spelling or errors (conjecture). The plain-text opening 「コロクニシ」 yields no meaning.
- Cross-check: frame 0025 of the same item is the draft of a reply from the Chief of the General Affairs Department of the General Staff Office to the Chief of Staff of the 12th Division (1904-01-27), saying that 「韓国五万分一地図五十部並韓国満洲百万分一地図二十部」 (50 copies of the 1:50,000 map of Korea and 20 copies of the 1:1,000,000 map of Korea and Manchuria) will be sent.
- Character count: kana 5 + digits 26 + kana 7 = 38 characters, 2 fewer than the 40 on the form (the reason is not known).

## 3. C09122909900 — 1902-03-28, Osaka, Ōyama → Major General Fukushima (partial reading)
- File: same as no. 1, 『明治３５年 電報綴 北清事件』. 2 frames (0078: decoded text; 0079: delivery slip). The draft of the corresponding outgoing telegram is C09122909800 (frame 0077), in which 「海軍」 and 「特別演習」 are boxed and carry furigana 「カイグン」 and 「トクベツエンシウ」.
- Delivery slip (frame 0079): sending office オウサカ (Osaka), No. 819, 28 March, 33 characters (consistent with 10 + 10 + 10 + 3).
- Text: 08 17 79 10 39 10 17 29 38 10 59 28 ｜ ソ（ノ）ケンキヨカス ｜ 80
- Reading: カ シ グ ン エ ン シ ウ ケ ン ガ ク → following the furigana of the outgoing telegram, 「カイグンエンシウケンガク」 (naval exercise observation). Decoded text (frame 0078): 「海軍演習ニ見学者出ス件 許可ス」 (Permission granted for sending observers to the naval exercise).
- One pair that does not agree: 17 in the 2nd pair. In the table イ = 19 and 17 is シ. The image also shows 七 (all three character judgments were 七). Taken to be an error by the writer or in transmission (conjecture).

## 4. C03020059400 — 1904-01, Division Commander Inoue → Minister of the Army (partial reading)
- File: Army Ministry Daijiki (records), 『明治３７年 満密大日記 明治３７年 １月』. Title: 「碇泊場司令部編成之件」 (Organization of the anchorage headquarters). 23 frames (0118–0140). The telegram is in frame 0119. Decoded text in the catalogue: 「辛碇泊場司令部ニ要スル下士以下及器具材料ハ門司補給廠支廠長ニ交付済…」.
- Digits in the text: 17 10 36 19 09 28 88 29 17 31 19 76 (kana follow)
- Reading: シ ン テ イ ア ク ゼ ウ シ レ イ ブ. Set beside the decoded text 「辛碇泊場司令部」 (シンテイハクジウシレイブ; 場 is spelled ジウ), 10 of 12 agree.
- Two pairs that do not agree: 09 (in the table ア; for the plain-text ハ it would be 04) and 88 (in the table ゼ; for the plain-text ジ it would be 68). In each case a single digit differs. The image shows 〇九 and 八八. Since 「九」 was suspected of being 「四」, it was set beside the 10 digits of the same writer and judged by Codex twice and Sonnet twice; in all four judgments the first candidate was 九 (`second/codex/runN1.txt`, `runN2.txt`). Taken to be an error by the writer or in transmission (conjecture).

## 5. C09122806100 — 1901-02-18, Hiroshima, Major General Manabe → Major General Tamura (unread)
- File: 『明治３４年 電報綴 北清事件』. 3 frames (0244: decoded text; 0245–0246: two telegram forms).
- Four groups of digits (14, 2, 8 and 6 digits) stand among the plain-text kana. From the decoded text 「石橋転任ノ事ニ就キ意見御尋ネナラバ一応山口中将ヘ御尋ネアリタシ病状ニ就テハ任地ハ適当ト考フ然カシ未ダ充分ナル服務ニハ堪ヘザルベシ」 and the surrounding plain text, the portion each group covers is determined (S2 = ニ, S3 = ニンチワ, S4 = フクム).
- Taking 2 digits as 1 kana, the lengths fit, but the same ニ appears as 91 and as 69, and the values of all 15 pairs differ. It cannot be read with the table of 1902. → [OPEN.en.md](OPEN.en.md)
