## Secondary sources
- Tomokiyo Satoshi, 「明治・大正期の陸軍暗号」, Cryptiana (https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/meijiarmy.htm). Periods of Yakugō (鑰号), Butsugō (物号), Kongō (坤号) and Tokugō (特号); the coordinate-type numeric ciphers of the Army of 1874 and 1894; the Army telegraph code (陸軍電信符号). Consulted 2026-10-20.
- Tomokiyo Satoshi, "Early Japanese Diplomatic Codes: 1874-1933", Cryptiana (https://cryptiana.web.fc2.com/code/meiji.htm). Consulted 2026-10-20.
